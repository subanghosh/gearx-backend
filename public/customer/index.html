Location Access Consent

ReDrivo collects and accesses your device's location data to enable:

• Finding Nearby Garages: Automatically search and display active service centers near your current location.
• Trip Routing & Proximity: Calculate exact pickup/drop distances to match your requests with nearby drivers.
• Live Driver Tracking: Track the assigned driver's movement relative to your pickup location.

Location data is accessed only when you are actively using the application. We do not use your location for advertising, nor do we share it with third parties.

Terms & Conditions

1. Vehicle Ownership & Authorization

By requesting a service, you warrant that you are the legal owner of the vehicle or have explicit permission from the legal owner to request service and entrust the vehicle to our drivers.


2. Inspection & Handover Protocol

Both the pickup and drop-off steps require a mandatory vehicle inspection, including an odometer reading check and a 360-degree walkaround photo/video capture. This visual log is used to verify the condition of the vehicle and avoid discrepancies. You agree to cooperate with the driver during this process.


3. Service Charges & Pricing

Pricing is calculated based on pickup/drop distances from the selected partner garage and any service/inspection fees. All charges are transparently listed in your booking dashboard prior to trip initiation.


4. Liability Limitation

ReDrivo and its partner garages maintain commercial transit insurance. In the rare event of damage during driver transit, liability is limited as per our transit policy terms. ReDrivo is not liable for personal belongings left inside the vehicle.

Privacy Policy & Consent Notice

Compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India)


1. Personal Data We Process

We collect and process your name, contact phone number, vehicle registration plates, and device GPS coordinates. We also record 360-degree walkaround videos and odometer readings at vehicle handover.


2. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your data strictly to verify vehicle ownership, find nearby partner garages, assign nearby drivers for pickup/drop, track vehicle transit live, and process service invoices.


3. Right to Withdraw Consent

You have the right to withdraw your consent for location tracking or data collection at any time. Withdrawal can be requested by contacting our support team or updating permissions in your profile. Please note that withdrawing consent may limit our ability to provide transit services.


4. Data Grievance Redressal Officer

For any grievances regarding your data, you can reach out to our Grievance Redressal / Data Protection Officer:

Grievance Officer & DPO: Rajesh Kumar
Email: grievance@redrivo.com / support@redrivo.com
