Location Access Consent

ReDrivo collects and accesses your device's location data to enable:

• Nearby Request Dispatch: Show you available pickup requests in your immediate vicinity.
• Transit Tracking: Provide live location updates to the customer and operations dashboard while you are driving the vehicle.

Background Location Access: Since you must be tracked during vehicle transit, location is accessed in the background while your status is set to "ONLINE", even if the screen is off or the app is minimized.

Terms & Conditions

1. Professional Conduct & Licensing

You agree to maintain a valid Indian driving license (DL) and verify that it remains in active, unexpired status. You must drive safely and comply with all local traffic laws during pickup and drop-off transits.


2. Strict Protocol Adherence

You are required to follow the 5-step handover protocol for both pickup (handover) and delivery. This includes taking a clear photo of the vehicle's odometer and recording a 360-degree walkaround video before taking possession or returning the vehicle. Falsification or skipping of these steps will result in suspension.


3. Background Location Tracking

You acknowledge that ReDrivo will track your location while you are set to ONLINE to manage dispatches and share live updates with customers. You agree to leave location permissions enabled.

Privacy Policy & Consent Notice

Compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India)


1. Data We Process

We collect and process your name, contact phone number, driving license number, PAN details, Aadhaar information (for identity verification), profile picture, and live GPS location coordinates.


2. Purpose of Processing

Your identification documents are processed for mandatory KYC verification before dispatching customer requests. Your location data is tracked live to calculate proximity to requests, manage trip dispatches, and show en-route progress to customers.


3. Right to Erasure & DPO

You can request correction or deletion of your personal records from ReDrivo's systems upon termination of your partnership, subject to regulatory retention laws. For any grievances, reach our Data Protection Officer:
